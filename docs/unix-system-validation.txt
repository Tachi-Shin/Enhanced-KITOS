Practical limits:
  No physical Pi 4 testing. Hardware build and QEMU validation are separate.
  Central boot-service modules remain pinned until reboot; updating their
    embedded resources requires rebuilding/replacing kernel8.img.
  Native TIMER/GPIO libraries containing EL1 instructions were build-checked;
    their execution was tested inside EKITOS/QEMU, not a macOS user process.
  Filesystem remains FAT32, without Unix permissions/UIDs/symlinks/hardlinks.
    /dev and /proc are reserved directories, not implemented devfs/procfs.
  Single-user system; administration syscalls have no UID/capability checks.
  Shell lacks pipelines, redirection, job control, globbing and general scripts.
    cd/export/unset/exit and process-management builtins remain in the shell.
  Existing USB class limits remain: Boot HID and BOT/SCSI storage; no UAS,
    SuperSpeedPlus, isochronous or all-device compatibility claim.
  See unix-system.md, ../sdk/README.md and usb-superspeed.md.
